    Welcome

    Selena Wang, PhD
    selewang@iu.edu

    Department of Biostatistics and Health Data Science
    HITS 3071
    410 W. 10th Street
    Indianapolis, IN 46202





    Dr. Wang is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Biostatistics and Health Data Science at Indiana University School of Medicine. She has an interdisciplinary background in biostatistics and computational psychology, with expertise in developing statistical and computational methods for complex, high-dimensional biomedical and networked data. Her research advances generative embedding, scalable methods and software for high-dimensional networked data, and Bayesian inference for brain and protein networks, with applications in disease subtyping, brain–behavior linkage, biomarker identification, and disease pathway discovery, particularly in Alzheimer's disease and other psychiatric and psychological conditions. Her work has been published in leading journals, including Nature Methods, Alzheimer's & Dementia, Medical Image Analysis, Psychometrika, Psychological Methods, and Human Brain Mapping. Dr. Wang has been named a William H. Gates Sr. Fellow by Alzheimer's Disease Data Initiative, a Research Career Institute in the Mental Health of Aging (CIMA) Scholar and a Research Education Component (REC) Scholar of the Indiana Alzheimer's Disease Research Center. Her methodological research program is supported by the Alzheimer's Disease Data Initiative and the National Institute on Aging.
