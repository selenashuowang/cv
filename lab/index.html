    Current Members

    Postdoctoral Fellow

    Dr. Jaeyoung Lee
    Dr. Jaeyoung Lee is a postdoctoral fellow in the lab. He received his doctoral training in Statistics at Virginia Tech, where his dissertation focused on supervised variational autoencoder frameworks for predictive modeling and association analysis. His research interests include heterogeneous data learning, deep generative models, variational inference, Bayesian methods, latent space models, dimension reduction, and statistical machine learning. In the lab, he develops statistical and machine learning methods for high-dimensional biomedical and neuroimaging data.

    Ph.D. Students

    Renhao Li
    Renhao Li is a Ph.D. student working with our team. His research focuses on statistical and computational methods for high-dimensional biomedical data, with interests in network modeling, machine learning, and neuroimaging applications.
    Luling Altan
    Luling Altan is a Ph.D. student in SWLab. Her research focuses on developing Bayesian methodology for complex networks, particularly brain and protein networks. She suspects that much of what we observe is a projection from a much higher-dimensional space, and that the most interesting part of a dataset often lies in its latent structure rather than the features we directly measure. With a background in mathematics, she is drawn to graph theory and its applications in neuroscience for this reason.
    In her spare time, she loves rock climbing, researching the art of raising cats, reading about Vedic astrology, photographing nebulae and galaxies, and predicting people's destinies with star charts.

    INGEN4DS Fellow / Research Assistant

    Luke Hawthorn is an INGEN4DS Fellow and research assistant working with our team. His research interests include computational and quantitative approaches to complex biomedical data. Through the INGEN4DS fellowship, he is gaining interdisciplinary research experience at the intersection of data science, genomics, and biomedical research.

    Research Assistants

    Ash Sharma
    Ash Sharma has a background in computer science and statistics. His research focuses on statistical and machine learning methods for high-dimensional neuroimaging and biological data, including latent-variable approaches to neuroimaging reliability and proteomic signatures of brain microstructural integrity.
    He presented his work on proteomic signatures of whole-brain white matter microstructural integrity using LatentSNA at the 2026 Alzheimer's Association International Conference (AAIC).
    Software: Contributed code fixes to the LatentSNA package, including ensuring the Sutheta prior is positive-definite, proper initialization before the MCMC loop, and error handling.
